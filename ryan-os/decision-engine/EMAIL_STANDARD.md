# Internal Load & Bid Email Standard

**Version 1.0.0** · Rendered by `engine.render_email()` · Governed by `defaults.json`

Every internal turnover email looks like this, from every agent, every time.
Consistency is the point: the estimating team should be able to read these at a
glance and know exactly where to find the margin, the equipment, and the open
questions.

---

## Recipients

Always all three, always on the same email:

- Ryan
- Veston
- estimating3@wahoocomfortsolutions.com

Set in `defaults.json → recipients.to`. Never hardcode them in a prompt.

## Subject

```
LOAD AND BID NEEDED: {Builder} & {Project}
```

Prefixes, applied by the engine:

| Prefix | When |
|---|---|
| `RUSH - ` | A deadline was stated, or rush language was detected |
| `HOLD - RYAN REVIEW - ` | Outcome is `ESCALATE_TO_RYAN` |

## Format rules

- **Plain text.** This gets pasted into Outlook. No markdown, no bold, no
  bullets that Outlook will mangle in a reply-all chain.
- Section headings on their own line, blank line between sections.
- Every assumption carries a confidence level and a source.
- The closing line is fixed and always last.

## Section order

1. `Project:` / `Builder:`
2. **Objective**
3. **Manual J Information**
4. **Bid Prep Information**
5. **Equipment Assumptions**
6. **Pricing Profile**
7. **Options Included**
8. **Builder Standing Instructions** — omitted when the builder has none
9. **Known Unknowns** — always present; reads "None" when there are none
10. **Assumption Confidence** — the table
11. **Attachments**
12. Closing line

## Closing line (fixed)

```
Please reply all when the Manual J is complete and again when the bid is complete.
```

## What "Known Unknowns" is for

It is the safety valve that lets the engine move fast. Every LOW-confidence
assumption lands there automatically, along with missing attachments and any
Ryan-review items. Nothing is hidden — but nothing waits, either.

If Known Unknowns says "None", the estimator can work the whole job from the
email without asking anyone a question. That is the target state, and it is what
a complete Builder Profile buys you.

## Relationship to the older `wcs-bid-request` skill

The `wcs-bid-request` skill produced a similar email from Ryan's dictated notes.
This standard supersedes it for **inbound builder requests** processed by the
Orchestrator. The differences that matter:

| | `wcs-bid-request` | This standard |
|---|---|---|
| Margin | 34% custom / 18% production | Builder Profile, else 35% / 30% / 35% |
| Source of truth | Ryan's notes | Builder Library + governed defaults |
| Confidence | not tracked | required on every assumption |
| Escalation | not modeled | four hard stops, everything else flags |

See `../governance/DECISION_LOG.md` entry **ADR-002** for the margin change.
